# Seam-center gates, pre-launch proof

Independent grader. These gates score a later worker's ApexCore seam move. They do not change the slicer. `validation/seam_fuse_gates/`, `scripts/`, `src/`, and `server/` are untouched.

Entry point: `bash validation/seam_center_gates/run_center_gates.sh BASE_DIR CAND_DIR`. Exit 0 only when every gate passes. The manifest check runs first (`sha256sum -c` from `validation/`).

## Forefoot index

A2 uses sfg_common layer numbers 79–117 inclusive. That index is the 0-based `;LAYER_CHANGE` count. Closed outer loops start at layer 1. On this BASE file:

- Layer 79: Z 52.694 mm. Layer 117: Z 77.414 mm. That is the brief's ~53–78 mm ball (G-code Z, the belt height).
- `along_mm` printed by A2 is the 45° arc `(Z - toe Z) * sqrt(2)` (71.76–106.72 mm on this band). It is a report, not the selector. `FOREFOOT_MODE` stays `layer`.

Measured on BASE ApexCore orthocad, 436 closed outer loops, 0 faces narrower than 3 mm (insole also 0). Worst center error is layer 111, seam X 174.606 vs center 138.541, **36.065 mm**. Largest adjacent seam step is L80→L81, **50.566 mm** (face-center step 0.064 mm). Heel jump L364→L365 is **15.540 mm**. insole_sample seams are all at X 140.000.

## What each gate scores

Thresholds are the brief's. Faces under 3 mm are exempt from A1/A2/A3 and counted. The exemption ceiling is the BASE count. The scripted-correct transform does not change face width, so that count equals the P2 count. Here both are 0.

- A1: seam X within 0.10 mm of that loop's bottom-face center (G1 `bottom_edge`, 0.25 mm of the loop's min Y).
- A2: A1 on orthocad layers 79–117.
- A3, as written: `|seam X step| <= |face-center X step| + 0.10` for adjacent closed layers. A constant X fails A1 and does not fail this form of A3, because the seam step is 0. P3(a) therefore clamps X to the bottom-face point nearest 140, which fails both.
- A4: layer-change end X within 0.6 mm of that layer's seam. A layer with a closed loop must also be within G1's 0.55 mm of the bottom. Open-only caps use G1's LC-open rule (count ≤ BASE). BASE already has one open cap 2.799 mm off the bottom (layer 437); the seam move does not own that point.
- A5: insole ApexCore seam X within 0.01 mm of BASE.
- A6: wall vertices on the BASE path within 0.005 mm; extra vertices only at a chain start (the collinear split); E within 0.5% plus 0.0002 mm of filament. The absolute term is the 0.001 mm XY rounding of a split (`E/mm * 0.001`). Feeds within 0.5 mm/min of the BASE segment. Retract/prime/wipe sequences match, rotation allowed. Fan/temp command list identical. A matching fan line is printed as `-1`.
- A7: `toolpath_seconds` and `net_e_mm` ≤ BASE + 0.5% on both ApexCore files.
- A8: ApexLight bytes match after dropping at most one `; generated by ... on <date>` line.
- A9: `ctc_frozen.analyze` perimeter, surface, and seam-block crossings are 0.
- G1: frozen `g1_seam_bottom.py`, BASE as `--ref`.

`scg_common.py` pins `sfg_common.py`, `ctc_frozen.py`, and `g1_seam_bottom.py` to the hashes in `validation/seam_fuse_gates/SHA256SUMS`.

P3e slows every closed-outer extrusion that is already at least 10 mm and faster than F1200 (orthocad 616 moves, 11660.4 mm; insole 182 moves, 6279.4 mm). One extra 10 mm at F1200 instead of F1560 is about 0.12 s, 0.002% of the orthocad toolpath, which is inside the 0.5% time gate, so a single 10 mm change would fail A6 and pass A7. The candidate above is the one that fails both, and the measured percents are the ones in the table.

P2 is built by `scripted_correct.py`: collinear split of each closed outer to its bottom-face center, inner walls rotated with the post-processor's belt-side anchor, travels and wipes moved onto the new point, then `postprocess_g2b.clear_perimeter_crossings` (imported, not edited) for any seam travel the move caused to cut a wall. Orthocad stats: moved_outer 334, moved_inner 202, skipped 102, inner_miss 79. insole stats: moved_outer 0 (already at X 140). ApexLight files are byte copies of BASE.

Bad candidates are built by `bad_candidates.py`. Neither script is in the frozen manifest.

## CI

`.github/workflows/seam-center-gates.yml` is additive. On this branch, and on pull requests that touch the gate or proof paths, it checks the manifest, `py_compile`, and `bash -n`. It does not slice and it does not run the proof. No existing job is renamed or set to continue on error.
